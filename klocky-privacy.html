Klocky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy describes Klocky: Real Clockwork Sim (also called Klocky), published by CAT TWO GAMES PTY LTD, on Android, iOS/iPadOS and Windows.

Gameplay and local storage

Klocky does not require an account with the developer. Game progress, settings and the mobile full-game unlock status are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay records or purchase tokens. The current game does not include advertising or developer analytics SDKs. Device or platform backups are governed by the provider’s settings and policies.

Purchases

The first four machines in the mobile game are free. An optional one-time full-game unlock uses Google Play Billing on Android and Apple In-App Purchase through StoreKit on iPhone and iPad. These platforms process payment and account information under their own policies. The game receives product and purchase information needed to verify, acknowledge and restore access, and stores the unlock status locally. We do not receive your payment-card number or bank-account details from an in-app purchase. Apple and Google may provide order information through their developer consoles for payment administration, refunds and support. Steam purchases and downloads on Windows are handled by Valve.

Platform services

App stores, Steam and optional testing services such as TestFlight may process account, purchase, installation or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. Information they make available to us may be used to maintain the game, investigate technical issues or support purchases. This does not mean that Klocky runs its own analytics service.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond to your request and resolve issues, and retain them only as needed for support and applicable recordkeeping obligations. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary sensitive information.

Deleting information

You can remove local game data using your device’s app-storage controls or by deleting the relevant local files. This can erase progress and settings. A mobile full-game purchase can be restored through the game using the same Apple Account or Google Play account, subject to the platform’s purchase status and availability. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to records we must retain. Apple, Google and Valve manage data associated with their platform accounts and transactions.

Children

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through Klocky. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if these practices change.

Klocky 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 21 日

本政策适用于 CAT TWO GAMES PTY LTD 发行的《Klocky: Real Clockwork Sim》（简称 Klocky）的 Android、iOS/iPadOS 和 Windows 版本。

游戏与本地存储

Klocky 无需注册开发者账号。游戏进度、设置和手机版完整版解锁状态保存在你的设备上。我们没有接收游戏记录或购买令牌的自有服务器。当前游戏不包含广告或开发者统计分析 SDK。设备或平台提供的备份服务受其设置与隐私政策约束。

购买

手机版前四台机械可免费体验，其余机械可通过一次性应用内购买解锁。Android 使用 Google Play Billing，iPhone 和 iPad 使用 Apple StoreKit 应用内购买。平台依据自身政策处理支付和账号信息。游戏接收核验、确认和恢复购买所需的商品及购买信息，并在本地保存解锁状态。我们不会通过应用内购买获得你的银行卡号或银行账户资料。Apple 和 Google 可能通过开发者后台向我们提供订单信息，用于支付管理、退款和客服。Windows 版的 Steam 购买及下载由 Valve 处理。

平台服务

应用商店、Steam 和 TestFlight 等可选测试服务可能按照各自的设置和政策处理账号、购买、安装或诊断信息。平台向我们提供的信息可用于维护游戏、排查技术问题或处理购买问题。这不代表 Klocky 运行自有统计分析服务。平台隐私政策链接见上方英文部分。

客服

如果你通过电子邮件联系我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址以及你主动提供的信息或附件。这些资料仅用于回复和处理问题，并在客服需要及适用记录保存义务所要求的期限内保留。我们不会出售这些资料或将其用于广告。请勿发送密码、银行卡信息或不必要的敏感资料。

删除信息

你可以通过设备的应用存储设置或删除相关本地文件清除游戏数据，这可能导致进度和设置丢失。手机版购买可在游戏中使用同一 Apple 账号或 Google Play 账号恢复，但仍受平台购买状态和服务可用性限制。你可以联系我们申请删除客服往来资料，但依法必须保存的记录除外。平台账号和交易数据由 Apple、Google 或 Valve 管理。

儿童

我们不会有意通过 Klocky 向儿童索取个人信息。如你认为儿童通过客服请求向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

更新与联系

如数据处理方式发生变化，我们将更新本页面。
