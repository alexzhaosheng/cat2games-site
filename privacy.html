MOMENT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy describes the Android, iOS/iPadOS and Windows versions of MOMENT: A Clockwork Melody, published by CAT TWO GAMES PTY LTD.

Gameplay and local storage

The game does not require a developer account. Progress, settings, custom levels and the mobile full-game unlock status are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay records, custom levels or purchase tokens. The current game does not include advertising or developer analytics SDKs. Backups provided by your device or platform are governed by that provider's settings and policies.

Purchases

On Android, the optional full-game unlock uses Google Play Billing. On iPhone and iPad, it uses Apple In-App Purchase through StoreKit. These platforms process payment and account information under their own policies. The game receives the product and purchase information needed to verify and restore access, and stores the unlock status locally. We do not receive your card number or bank account details from an in-app purchase. The platforms may provide order information through their developer consoles for payment administration, refunds and support. Windows purchases and downloads through Steam are handled by Valve under Steam's policies.

Platform privacy policies

Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Valve: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

Sharing levels

The game can export and import level share codes. You choose whether and where to share a code. The game does not automatically upload your levels to us or provide a public content feed. Other services you choose to use have their own privacy policies.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for support and applicable recordkeeping requirements, and do not sell it or use it for advertising. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary sensitive information.

Deleting information

You can remove local game information using your device's app-storage controls or by deleting the relevant local game data. Removing app data can erase progress, settings and custom levels. A mobile full-game purchase can be restored using the same Apple Account or Google Play account through Restore Purchase, subject to the platform's purchase status and availability. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to records we must retain. Apple, Google and Valve manage data associated with your platform account and transactions.

Children

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if these practices change.
