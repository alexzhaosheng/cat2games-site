MOMENT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy describes the Android version of MOMENT: A Clockwork Melody, published by CAT TWO GAMES PTY LTD.

Gameplay and local storage

The game does not require a developer account. Progress, settings, custom levels and the full-game unlock status are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay records, custom levels or purchase tokens. The game does not include advertising or developer analytics SDKs.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock uses Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and account information under its own policies. The game receives purchase information needed to verify, acknowledge and restore the unlock, and stores the unlock status locally. We do not receive your card number or bank account details from an in-app purchase. Google Play may provide us with order information through its developer console for payment administration, refunds and support.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing levels

The game can export level share codes. You choose whether and where to share those codes; the game does not automatically upload them to us. Services you use to share a code have their own privacy policies.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for support and applicable recordkeeping requirements, and do not sell it or use it for advertising. Please avoid sending passwords, card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Deleting information

You can delete local game information using Android's app storage controls. Clearing game data resets local progress and settings; purchased access can be restored through the same Google Play account. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any records we must retain. Google manages data associated with your Google Play account and transactions.

Children

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if these practices change. For privacy questions, contact cat2gamesstudio@gmail.com.
