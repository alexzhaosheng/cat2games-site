01 / MUSICAL PUZZLES

MOMENT

A Clockwork Melody

Coming soon on Steam

Solve a puzzle. Compose a melody.

Place clockwork launchers, tune their timing and guide falling balls along carefully planned routes. Every collision plays a note. As the machine comes together, your solution becomes a piece of music you can see and hear.

02 / THE JOY OF CLOCKWORK

Klocky

Tooth by tooth, wheel by wheel.

Planned for March 2027 on Steam

Slow down. Make time move.

Under the lamp lies an old drawing. Fit gears, escapements and balance wheels into place, then hear your machine's first tick. Follow hand-drawn assembly plans and discover the history and ingenuity inside each mechanism.

Wishlist on Steam↗

Windows · more than 30 machines planned for the full game

01

Every tooth drives the next

Gear ratios, escapements, cams and planetary gears work together. The parts you assemble determine how the mechanism moves.

02

A book for every machine

Explore mechanical history and principles through engraving-style illustrations, then turn the page and build from the assembly drawings.

03

Bring famous clocks to life

Assemble world-famous clocks such as the Prague Astronomical Clock with your own hands, alongside astronomical instruments, musical mechanisms and automata.

Klocky is in development. The full game is planned to include more than 30 machines; images and trailers show the game in development.
